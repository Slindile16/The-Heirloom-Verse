# Free campaign audio preview

The separate narrated MP4 is at `../dist/assets/heirloom-verse-campaign-narrated.mp4`. The current homepage video has not been replaced. `soundtrack-preview.m4a` contains the same audio mix for quick review.

Created locally without subscription services: Microsoft Hazel desktop synthetic narration, an original synthesised broken-chord instrumental bed, and the approved film's existing audio quietly underneath. This is a synthetic-voice preview, not a professional human voice recording. No separately recorded boutique or ribbon foley has been added.

Narration is slightly accelerated to fit, begins after 0.65 seconds, and finishes before the 40-second cut ends. The final mix is peak-limited and fades out. The original downloaded video and previously integrated video are unchanged.
