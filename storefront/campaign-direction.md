## Original film direction
One approximately 30–40 second live-action-style film. Warm intimate window light, real skin texture, ivory silk, burgundy details, champagne gold; restrained transitions and no rapid cuts.
1. 0–5s: in a refined boutique, a sales associate hands an adult woman an ivory paper bag over the counter. Burgundy HV faces camera; burgundy ribbon handles. No claims of a real physical store: fictional campaign setting.
2. 5–12s: at home, the same woman lifts out the ivory box, unties the burgundy ribbon, opens the outer box and then opens the burgundy case to reveal earrings.
3. 12–18s: same woman puts on the earrings at a mirror, natural hands and realistic jewellery scale.
4. 18–26s: a man and woman open a necklace gift; he gently fastens the necklace behind her neck.
5. 26–33s: close-up of their hands finding each other, showing coordinated matching bracelets and rings from the actual catalogue.
6. 33–36s: restrained gold HV on burgundy closing frame.

Use actual catalogue product references. Keep casting, wardrobe, lighting, logos and packaging consistent between shots. Assemble short generated clips; inspect hands, clasps, jewellery design and counts before integration. Deliver landscape H.264 MP4, web-optimised, plus a mobile crop that keeps jewellery visible; no slideshow presented as live action. Add a shot-based descriptive transcript if needed, and licensed or generated instrumental audio only.
